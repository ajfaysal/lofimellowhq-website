Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled on the official LofiMellowHQ website.

Information Collection

The website does not require account creation for listening and browsing. If you use the contact form, the information you submit is used only to respond to your message.

How Information Is Used

Submitted contact details are used for communication regarding your inquiry, collaboration request, or business topic. Information is not sold or shared for unrelated marketing.

Analytics and Performance

Basic anonymous traffic analytics may be used to understand website performance and improve the listening experience. No sensitive personal profiling is performed.

External Platforms

Links to streaming and social platforms open on external websites that maintain their own privacy practices. Please review those policies when visiting third-party services.
